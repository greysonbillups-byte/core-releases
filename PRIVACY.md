# Privacy Policy — CORE

**Version 1.0 — Effective 15 September 2026**

## The short version

CORE runs on your computer. Your match data, team and player names, rosters, brackets, results, overlays, images, settings and saved replays stay on that computer and are never sent anywhere.

There is no account, no analytics, no telemetry, and no usage tracking of any kind.

## Who this is from

CORE is made by Greyson Billups. For anything in this policy, contact **greysonbillups@gmail.com**.

## What CORE stores on your computer

Everything you create in CORE is kept locally, in your user profile folder:

- Match data, schools, teams, players, brackets, schedules and results
- Overlay files, images, logos and theme colours
- Saved replays
- Application settings and hotkey bindings
- Your licence key, encrypted using your operating system's credential storage where available

None of this is transmitted, and there is no server that could receive it.

---

## What CORE sends, and to whom

CORE makes exactly two kinds of outbound request.

### Licence verification — Lemon Squeezy

Only if you have a paid licence. Sent to Lemon Squeezy, which provides licensing and payment:

- **When you activate:** your licence key, and a label for the computer made up of its hostname and operating system (for example, "DESKTOP-7F2A (win32)"). This label is what lets you tell your machines apart when releasing a seat.
- **When your licence is checked, or you release a computer:** your licence key and the activation identifier issued at activation.

This exists to enforce how many computers a licence covers. Nothing about your matches, teams or usage is included.

### Software updates — GitHub

CORE checks for a new version when it starts and roughly every six hours after that. The request goes to GitHub, which hosts the downloads.

As with visiting any website, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request information. No licence key, no identifier, and nothing about you or your use of CORE is sent with it.

### Everything else is local

CORE's connections to OBS Studio, and to game data sources such as the Valorant or Counter-Strike local APIs, are to your own computer. So are the HTTP and WebSocket servers CORE runs to feed your overlays. None of that traffic leaves your machine.

---

## What CORE does not do

- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage statistics
- No advertising and no third-party trackers
- No account or sign-up
- No collection of anything from the overlays you broadcast

---

## Purchases

Purchases are handled by **Lemon Squeezy**, acting as merchant of record. They collect the information needed to take payment — typically your name, email address, billing country and payment details — under their own privacy policy.

Payment details are never seen or stored by Greyson Billups. Order and licence records, which include your email address, are visible in the Lemon Squeezy dashboard and are used only to support your licence.

---

## Keeping and deleting your data

Licence and order records are kept while your licence is active and for as long as needed for accounting.

To ask what is held about you, to correct it, or to request deletion, email **greysonbillups@gmail.com**. Requests are answered within 30 days. Deleting licence records may end your ability to activate CORE.

Data held locally on your computer is under your control, and uninstalling CORE or deleting its profile folder removes it.

---

## Children

CORE is intended for use by schools, colleges and organisations running esports broadcasts. It is not directed at children under 13, and no information is knowingly collected from them.
