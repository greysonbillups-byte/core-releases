# Privacy Policy — CORE

**Version 1.1 — Effective 24 September 2026**

## The short version

CORE runs on your computer. Your match data, team and player names, rosters, brackets, results, overlays, images, settings and saved replays stay on that computer and are never sent to us or anyone else.

There is no account, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no usage tracking in the app. The only personal information we handle is what's needed to sell and verify a paid licence. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## What CORE stores on your computer

Everything you create in CORE is kept locally, in your user profile folder:

- Match data, schools, teams, players, brackets, schedules and results
- Overlay files, images, logos and theme colours
- Saved replays
- Application settings and hotkey bindings
- Your licence key, encrypted using your operating system's credential storage where available

None of this is transmitted, and we run no server that could receive it.

---

## What CORE sends, and to whom

The app makes exactly two kinds of outbound internet request.

### Licence verification: Lemon Squeezy

This happens only if you have a paid licence. The request goes to Lemon Squeezy, which provides licensing and payment:

- **When you activate:** your licence key, and a label for the computer made up of its hostname and operating system (for example, "DESKTOP-7F2A (win32)"). This label lets you tell your machines apart when releasing a seat. If your computer's name includes a person's name, that name will appear in your licence records; you can rename the computer before activating if you prefer.
- **When your licence is checked, or you release a computer:** your licence key and the activation identifier issued at activation.

As with any internet request, Lemon Squeezy also receives your IP address. This exists only to enforce how many computers a licence covers. Nothing about your matches, teams, players or usage is included.

### Software updates: GitHub

CORE checks for a new version when it starts and roughly every six hours after that. The request goes to GitHub, which hosts the downloads.

As with visiting any website, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request information. No licence key, no identifier, and nothing about you or your use of CORE is sent with it.

### Everything else is local

CORE's connections to OBS Studio, to Stream Deck and hotkey controls, and to any game data sources that run on your own computer stay on your machine. So do the HTTP and WebSocket servers CORE runs to feed your overlays: these listen only on the local computer (127.0.0.1) and are not reachable from other machines on your network. None of that traffic leaves your computer.

## Student and school data

CORE is built so that schools can use it without sharing student information with us.

- Student names, gamertags, photos, rosters, results and any other information you enter stay on your institution's computers. We never receive, access, store or process them.
- Because we receive no student information, we do not hold education records under FERPA, and we do not collect personal information from children under COPPA.
- The only information we receive is licence and purchase information about the person or institution that buys CORE (see "Purchases").

On request, we will complete a district or institutional data privacy agreement, or provide a written statement confirming the above.

## Purchases

Purchases are handled by **Lemon Squeezy**, acting as merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy collects the information needed to take payment, typically your name, email address, billing address or country, and payment details, under its own privacy policy.

We never see or store payment card details. We can see order and licence records in the Lemon Squeezy dashboard, including your name, email address, what you bought, and the computer labels described above. We use them only to provide and support your licence, to respond to you, and to keep the records required for tax and accounting. We will only email you about your licence or support requests, unless you have separately agreed to receive product news.

## Legal basis (EU, EEA and UK)

If you are in the EU, EEA or UK, we rely on these legal bases:

- **Contract:** processing licence and order records to provide the licence you bought.
- **Legitimate interests:** enforcing activation limits and preventing licence misuse.
- **Legal obligation:** keeping purchase records for tax and accounting.

Lemon Squeezy and GitHub may process information in the United States and other countries. They rely on their own recognised transfer safeguards (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework), described in their privacy policies.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Licence and order records are kept while your licence is active, and afterwards for as long as required for tax and accounting (generally up to seven years).

You can ask what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it, delete it, or give you a copy. Email **core.esportsbroadcast@gmail.com**. We will respond within 30 days, and we honour these requests wherever you live. Deleting licence records may end your ability to activate CORE, and we may keep records we are legally required to retain. If you are in the EU, EEA or UK, you also have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Data held locally on your computer is under your control. Uninstalling CORE or deleting its profile folder removes it.

## Security

Your licence key is encrypted using your operating system's credential storage where available. Licence and update requests are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). CORE's local overlay servers listen only on your own computer; see the End User Licence Agreement for details.

---

## Children

CORE does not collect personal information from anyone who uses the app, including children. Licences must be bought by an adult or by an institution, and we do not knowingly receive purchase or licence information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
